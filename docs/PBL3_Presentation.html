01 · Research problem

Audio and video do not always deserve equal trust

Challenge

  • Manipulation may affect the face, voice, or both.
  • Compression, blur, noise, and missing faces can weaken one stream.
  • Synchronization mismatch is informative but is not a fake/real verdict.

Project question

Does a lightweight reliability estimator improve detection over unimodal and equal-weight fusion baselines, especially when modality quality differs?

The gate is evaluated empirically; its benefit is not assumed.02 / 07

06 · Conclusion and next work

The implementation works; the reliability hypothesis remains open

Current evidence does not show that reliability-aware fusion improves generalization. The primary test split remains untouched.

NowTrain longer; repeat seeds; inspect gate weights and modality losses.
ThenRun degradation, missing-modality, and held-out manipulation evaluation.
FinalSelect and calibrate on validation; freeze the checkpoint; evaluate test once.
